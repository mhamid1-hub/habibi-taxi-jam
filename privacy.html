Privacy Policy for Habibi Taxi Jam

Last updated: 24/10/2026

Habibi Taxi Jam ("the app") is made by Mohamed Abdelhamid ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

No account, no personal details

The app has no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.

Game progress stays on your device

Your level, stars, coins, lives and purchases are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interactions. On iOS we ask your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent through Google's consent form. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own, which handles an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad tracking in your device settings, refuse tracking when asked, and delete the app at any time.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's practices change.
