Privacy Policy for Habibi Mummy

Last updated: 31/10/2026

Habibi Mummy ("the app") is made by Mohamed Abdelhamid ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No accounts, no personal details

The app has no sign-up and no sign-in. You do not enter your name, email, phone number or contacts. We do not ask for your birth date.

Game data stays on your device

Your progress, settings and purchases-related unlocks are saved on your device only. The app also stores an age band: either "under 13" or "13 or older". It never stores your birth date or exact age. We have no server that holds your game data. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

Children and the age band

The app is a family-friendly game for a general audience. When you first open the app you choose your age band. We use it to decide how ads and purchases are handled, as described below. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we apply extra protections to players in the under-13 band.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Two kinds of ad appear:

  • Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. You are never forced to watch one.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between levels, shown only after a few levels and not more often than a set interval.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), advertising data, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. What AdMob does with this information is set out in Google's own policies, which you can read in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Players under 13: we ask AdMob to show non-personalised ads only, to treat the app as child-directed, and to show only ads suitable for a general audience. We do not show Apple's tracking permission prompt to this group.

Players 13 or older: ads may be personalised, but only with consent. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we show Google's consent form first. On iOS, we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any personalised ad. If you say no, the ads you see are not personalised.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. To check which purchases you own and to restore them on a new device, we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat works with an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. It does not use your name or email. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Purchases for players under 13 are protected by a parental gate: a simple grown-up check that must be passed before any buy or restore action.

Analytics and selling of data

The app does not include its own analytics SDK. Google AdMob may collect app interaction data to measure and improve ad delivery, as described in Google's policies linked above. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose beyond running the game and showing ads as described here.

Your choices

  • Decline the tracking permission on iOS, or change it later in your device's privacy settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.
  • Watch or skip rewarded ads. You choose each time.
  • Delete the app at any time. This removes the game data stored on your device.

Purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat are not removed by uninstalling the app. If you want help with a data request, contact us below.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
