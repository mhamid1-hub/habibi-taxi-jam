About the game

Habibi Mummy is a family-friendly platformer set in Egyptian tombs. The mummy runs on its own: tap to jump, lash robbers with a bandage, swing on golden hooks, and grab Egyptian street-food power-ups. There are 150 levels, and you can unlock skins in the shop.

Contact

How do I restore my purchases?

On the home screen, tap the shop (bag) button, then Restore Purchases. This works on the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If your purchase still does not appear, check that you are signed in to the same store account and try again after a few minutes.

My purchase is not showing

First use Restore Purchases, as above. Purchases are checked with your store account, so the store account you bought with must be the one signed in on the device. If it still does not show, email us with the date of the purchase and the device you used.

There is no sound

Check that the phone's silent switch (iPhone) or media volume is not muted. Then open the gear icon in the game and check that Sound is on and the sound effects and music volumes are not at zero.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. There is no cloud save or account. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Reinstalling starts a fresh game.

Ads

The game shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional ads between some levels. You can decline the tracking prompt on iPhone at any time in your device's privacy settings.
