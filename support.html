My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. There is no cloud backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

I bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack and lost it

Open the shop in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. If restoring does not work, email us and include the receipt from Apple.

Coin purchases on a new device

Coin packs and the coins from the Starter Pack are consumables. They are not restored on a new device.

Ads

Remove Ads stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay optional. You choose whether to watch one.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
