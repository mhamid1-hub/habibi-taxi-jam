Privacy Policy for Habibi Beit

Last updated: 15/11/2026

Habibi Beit ("the app") is made by Mohamed Abdelhamid ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

The app does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet.

No accounts, no personal details

There is no sign-up and no sign-in. You do not enter a name, email address, phone number, birth date or contacts.

Game data stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, boosters, language choice and settings (sound and haptics) are saved on your device only. We have no server, and the app sends nothing from your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also clear it at any time with "Reset progress" in Settings.

No network use

The app makes no network requests. It works offline, and it does not contact us, Apple, Google or any other service.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no advertisements. It does not use the advertising identifier, and it does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, because it does not track you.

Purchases

The app has no in-app purchases. Every level and feature is available without payment.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game. Because it collects no personal information, it has nothing to share or delete for children or for anyone else.

Your choices

  • Reset your progress at any time in Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
