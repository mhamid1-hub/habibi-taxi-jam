About the game

Habibi Beit is a cosy tidy-up puzzle set in Teta's house. Clear each pile of household items, earn repairs, and restore five rooms. There are 100 levels, three boosters for hard levels, and English and Arabic language options. The game has no ads, no purchases and no accounts, and it works offline.

How do I change the language?

On the home screen, tap the gear icon, then choose English or العربية under Language.

There is no sound

Check that the phone's silent switch (iPhone) or media volume is not muted. Then open the gear icon in the game and check that Sound is on.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. There is no cloud save or account. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Reinstalling starts a fresh game.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the gear icon on the home screen, then choose Reset progress and confirm. This cannot be undone.

I am stuck on a level

When the tray is full and no match is possible, the level is lost. Use Try again, or use a booster: put back returns the last item in the tray, shuffle rearranges the pile, and extra slot adds one place in the tray for that level.
